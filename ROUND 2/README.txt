SYNTHETIC AIR QUALITY INSIGHT DATASET — ALL SEASONS
===================================================
All records are synthetic and are intended for educational practice only.

Files:
- air_quality.csv: timestamp, station, PM2.5, PM10, NO2, AQI
- stations.csv: station location and land-use type
- weather.csv: wind, temperature, humidity, rain
- traffic.csv: traffic volume and peak flag
- calendar.csv: weekday, holiday, season

Coverage:
One week of hourly data in each of four seasons: Winter (January),
Summer (April), Monsoon (July), and Post-Monsoon (October), 2026.
Six synthetic stations.

Injected data-quality issues:
- Missing pollution and weather readings
- Duplicate air-quality records
- Inconsistent station-name spellings
- Extreme sensor values

Use these files to demonstrate data cleaning and EDA. Findings must be
described as patterns in synthetic data, not real-world city measurements.
